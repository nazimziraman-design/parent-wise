Data Deletion Instructions

Parent Wise Publisher does not collect personal data from visitors or followers. The only data it holds are access tokens and statistics for the owner's own accounts, stored on the owner's computer.

To request deletion of any data related to you, send a direct message to @parentwise__ on Instagram with the subject "Data deletion". Requests are handled within 30 days.

You can also remove the app at any time: Facebook Settings, Business Integrations, select "Parent Wise Publisher", then Remove.

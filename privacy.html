Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

"Parent Wise Publisher" is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Parent Wise brand (Instagram: @parentwise__). It is used only to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own Instagram, Facebook Page and YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Meta (Instagram and Facebook): access tokens for the owner's Page and Instagram professional account, used only to publish posts and read basic post insights (reach, saves, shares) for the owner's own content.
  • YouTube: an OAuth token for the owner's own channel, used only to upload videos and read the owner's own video statistics.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of visitors, followers or other users. It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Storage and retention

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Statistics obtained through the YouTube API are refreshed at least every 30 days, and data older than 30 days is deleted. If access is revoked, related data is deleted within 7 days.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children. Published content does not show identifiable children.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
